Questions, answered

Know before you trade

Prediction markets reward correct outcomes, but every position carries risk.

What is a prediction market?

A market where participants take positions on verifiable future outcomes. Prices can reflect the crowd's current estimate of probability.

Is this page processing real trades?

No. Live price and network data are real, while orders and positions on this static preview are simulations.

How would a market resolve?

Each market identifies its resolution criteria and source before trading begins. The verified result determines the winning outcome.

Why use Stellar?

Stellar offers fast ledger settlement and low network fees, making it a strong foundation for frequent, small-value market activity.
